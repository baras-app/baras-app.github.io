## Features Overview

BARAS (Battle Analysis and Raid Assessment System) is a modern combat log parser for Star Wars: The Old Republic. It provides real-time overlays during gameplay and detailed post-combat analysis.

---

## Real-Time Overlays

BARAS provides [18 customizable overlays](/features/overlays) for monitoring combat performance in real-time:

- **Metrics** - DPS, healing, damage taken, threat meters for your group
- **Personal Stats** - Your individual performance at a glance
- **Boss Health** - Track boss HP during encounters
- **Encounter Timers** - Countdown timers for boss abilities and mechanics
- **Effects Tracking** - Monitor buffs, debuffs, cooldowns, and DOTs
- **Raid Frames** - Group health and status display
- **Alerts** - Notifications for important events

All overlays can be independently positioned, resized, and configured.

---

## Data Explorer

The [Data Explorer](/features/data-explorer) provides detailed post-combat analysis:

- **Performance Charts** - DPS, HPS, and DTPS graphs over time
- **Detailed Breakdowns** - Damage, healing, and threat by ability and target
- **Phase Analysis** - Filter metrics to specific encounter phases
- **Combat Log** - Searchable raw event viewer
- **Encounter History** - Review all parsed encounters from your logs

---

## Encounter Builder

The [Encounter Builder](/features/encounter-builder) lets you customize how encounters are tracked:

- **Entity Roster** - Define which NPCs belong to each encounter
- **Phases** - Create phases to segment fights for analysis
- **Timers** - Configure countdown timers for boss abilities
- **Counters** - Track encounter state for complex mechanics
- **Challenges** - Define custom performance metrics

All encounter definitions are configuration-driven and shareable.

---

## Effects Tracker

The [Effects Tracker](/features/effects-tracker) monitors buffs, debuffs, and cooldowns:

- **Buff/Debuff Tracking** - Monitor personal and group effects
- **Cooldown Tracking** - Track ability cooldowns
- **DOT Tracking** - Monitor damage-over-time effects on multiple targets
- **Alerts** - Get notified when effects apply or expire

---

## Key Features

### Fast and Performant

Parse entire raid nights in sub-second speeds. BARAS is optimized for performance and can run for extended sessions without memory issues.

### Fully Customizable

Every overlay, timer, and encounter definition can be configured to match your preferences. Nothing is hardcoded.

### Configuration-Driven

Share encounter definitions and overlay profiles with other players. Import community configurations or create your own.

### Cross-Platform

Native support for Windows, macOS, and Linux. No Wine, no hacks - BARAS runs natively on all major platforms.
